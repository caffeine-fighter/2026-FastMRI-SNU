5. Areas of Improvement
. Not enough parameter sweep (Especially for Knee)
- Due to time constraint for training 4 models, parameter sweep was insufficient
- The model performed well enough for brain, but not so much for knee
VRAM was not fully utilized (5~10% remained)

. Historical Feature

- While the paper demonstrated that this feature yields substantial performance gains, the training
diverged constantly when it was applied

. Additional Refinement Net
- The paper indicated that incorporating additional image refinement networks, such as NAFNet,
could further improve performance

3. MoE
Mixture of Experts (MoE)
MoE type: 4x / 8x, brain / knee
Our initial decision: Don't use MoE! Use a single model.
Why?
1. using MoE didn't have much effect on improving SSIM score.
2. (generalization) each expert model could overfit to training data.
however...
We initially trained the model with equispaced mask functions,
and we assumed that the private dataset would also use equispaced mask.
If private dataset contains other types of mask:
trained model would be vulnerable.
Solution: train another model with random mask, then use MoE.
